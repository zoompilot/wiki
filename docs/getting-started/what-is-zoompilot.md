# What is zoompilot?

zoompilot is free, open-source software that helps your car steer and
manage cruise speed. It runs on a supported comma device connected to your car.
{ .zp-lede }

It builds on sunnypilot and openpilot by comma.ai.

## what it adds

Speed-dependent steering learning benefits supported cars that use
torque-based steering. Mazda gets additional tuning matched to its
steering hardware, plus curve-control improvements and alert fixes.
[Explore the features](../features/index.md).

zoompilot runs on sunnypilot-supported cars. Available improvements
depend on the steering system and hardware; start with [compatibility](supported-cars.md).

## what you need to know

- **You drive the car.** Watch the road and be ready to steer and brake.
- **Hardware still limits steering.** Some cars cannot steer at low speeds or through sharp curves.
- **Optional features can change your cruise speed.** You choose which to enable.
- **Experimental speed control is separate.** On Mazda it turns off automatic emergency braking and forward collision warning.

Read [Safety](../safety.md) before using zoompilot.
