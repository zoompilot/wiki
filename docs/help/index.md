## ask for help

Post in [zoompilot Discord](https://discord.gg/jFWkHC2uhh) with your car,
comma model, software version, exact alert, and what happened.
If you have a recording, include a [route ID](share-a-route.md) and the
time of the problem. Help is provided by the community.
