# Help

Something wrong, or a word you do not know? Start with the page that
matches what you need. If none of them fixes it, the
[zoompilot Discord](https://discord.gg/jFWkHC2uhh) is where people help.

## Still stuck?

Post in the [zoompilot Discord](https://discord.gg/jFWkHC2uhh) with
three things: your car and model year, your zoompilot release, and a
[route ID](share-a-route.md). Bugs can also go to the
[zoompilot issue tracker](https://github.com/zoompilot/zoompilot/issues).
